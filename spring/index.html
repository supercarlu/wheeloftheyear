Season I of IV

Vår — Spring

The return of light. The ground remembers warmth. Each week something new breaks through — and we mark every turning with fire, food, and flowers.

March · April · May

In the kitchen

Forced rhubarb, new asparagus, spring lamb, fresh herbs, radishes, the first strawberries

For the home

Birch branches, pussy willow, daffodils, tulips, lilac, wildflowers and flower crowns

The mood

Awakening. The days lengthen visibly now. Open windows, light meals, fire at night

20 Mar
Pan-European · Astronomical

Spring Equinox — Vårjevndøgn

The exact moment when light and dark are equal — and from here the light wins. Still cold in Oslo, but something in the air has shifted. We mark the turning with lamb, rhubarb cake, and by eating dinner in natural light.

30 Apr
Norse · Germanic

Walpurgis Night — Valborg

The night winter is declared over. Fire, noise, and the insistence that spring has arrived — regardless of the weather. Bonfires have been lit on this night across Scandinavia for longer than anyone can remember.

Easter Weekend — Påske

Four days with a different character each. A gathering walk, a simple fish dinner, a making day with natural egg dyes and torrijas, and a slow-roasted lamb on Sunday. The Spanish torrijas are our household tradition.

Syttende Mai — Constitution Day

Norway's great national celebration. The parade, the bunad, the flags, the ice cream. At home: a table of smørbrød, a bløtkake made the evening before, and the lilac in full bloom in the windows.
