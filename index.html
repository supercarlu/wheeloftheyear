The Wheel of
Our Year

A calendar of celebrations, traditions, and recipes for our home — following the turning of the seasons.

Every year the wheel turns — through light and dark, fire and snow, harvest and rest. These are the celebrations we keep in our home: rooted in Norse and Iberian tradition, shaped by the seasons of Norway, and made our own.
